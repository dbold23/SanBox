# Faculty outreach email

Send mid-September to early October. One PI per email, under 200 words,
plain text, no attachments beyond a link to the repo and a 2-page PDF.
Check the department's admissions page first; some (Cornell ECE) ask you
not to contact faculty before applying.

Subject: Prospective PhD applicant, lens-free holography + deep learning for colony detection

Dear Professor [Last name],

I am a [senior / master's student] in [major] at California State University,
Monterey Bay, and I plan to apply to the [program] PhD program for Fall 2027.
I am writing because your work on [one specific paper or system, cited by
title or one-line description] is close to what I have been building.

Over the past [N months] I developed a pipeline that reconstructs lens-free
in-line holograms with angular-spectrum propagation across a depth stack,
selects the focal plane automatically, and segments bacterial colonies with a
U-Net trained on a BCE + Dice loss. On [N] held-out images it reaches a Dice
of [X]. Code and a short write-up are here: [repo link].

The direction I want to pursue in a PhD is [one sentence, for example:
end-to-end reconstruction-and-detection networks that operate on the raw
time-lapse holograms, with calibrated uncertainty for clinical use]. That
seems to fit [something concrete about their group].

Are you planning to take a new PhD student in Fall 2027? If so, I would be
grateful for any advice on whether my background is a fit. I have attached a
two-page summary of the project.

Thank you for your time.

[Name]
[Major], CSUMB, expected graduation [month year]
